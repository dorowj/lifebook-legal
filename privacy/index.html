Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

LifeBook helps families turn their trip photos and stories into books. We built it to keep your memories private.

What stays on your device

  • Your photo library is read on your iPhone to find trips and build books. Photos are not uploaded to us to do this.
  • Voice stories you record are stored on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Location data comes from photo metadata and is used on-device to place trips on the Globe.

What we may receive

  • If you sign in with Apple: an anonymous identifier (and email only if you choose to share it).
  • If you order a printed book (when available): the book file, shipping address, and order details go to our print partner to make and ship it. Payment goes through Apple Pay; we never see full card numbers.
  • Basic crash/diagnostic info, only if you allow it in iOS settings.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your photos for advertising or to train AI models.
  • No third-party ad trackers.

Your choices

  • Change Photos, Location, Microphone, and Notification access anytime in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove its local data. Email us to delete any account data we hold.

Children

LifeBook is a family app meant to be managed by adults.

Hosting
