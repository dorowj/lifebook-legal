FAQ

Why don’t I see any trips?
LifeBook needs Photos access. Go to Settings → LifeBook → Photos → Full Access.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. Trips and books are built on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I order a printed book?
Printing is coming soon.

How do I record a voice story?
Open a trip, tap the mic, and allow Microphone access.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app; email us for any account data.
